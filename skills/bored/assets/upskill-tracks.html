Upskill Tracks

Pick a line, take the next stop, and tell it how it went. Claude reads your taps and adds the next lesson at the right level.

How updating works

  • Tap how a lesson went on the current stop. The next stop moves up, and you can undo a tap.
  • Next time you run /bored or say sync my tracks, Claude copies your taps into your taste profile and adds the next lesson: harder if you liked it, easier if it lost you.
  • Say start a track on system design (or any topic) for a new line. It shows up here as another card.
  • Saying it in chat works too: done #2, too easy.
